Should I evolve this Pokémon?

Choose a Pokémon to compare it with its next evolution. The recommendation uses the battle stats supplied in the case dataset.

Search by name or Pokédex number.

How the recommendation works

If a next evolution exists, the site compares total base battle stats (HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense and Speed). It recommends evolving when at least one next evolution has a higher total. If no next evolution exists in the dataset, the Pokémon is treated as a final form.

This is a simple case-study heuristic. The dataset does not contain level requirements, moves, held items, friendship or game-specific evolution conditions.

Data: supplied Pokémon case spreadsheet. Images: PokéAPI sprites repository.
